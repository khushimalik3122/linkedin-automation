## LinkedIn Post Draft

If AI can synthesize category research in thirty seconds, the traditional agency strategy deck is dead. 

Recent reporting from Marketing Dive underscores an inflection point for strategists: as generative tools commoditize research synthesis and automated reporting, clients refuse to pay for information aggregation. 

They are paying for conviction, cultural nuance, and commercial accountability.

When baseline execution is virtually frictionless, strategic value shifts toward decisions an algorithm cannot defend:

• Choosing what *not* to launch when martech allows you to spin up campaigns instantly.
• Translating brand reputation directly into commercial unit economics, not vanity impressions.
• Backing counter-intuitive creative bets that statistical averages actively advise against.

The strategists pulling ahead today aren't racing machines on speed. They are stepping into executive rooms as commercial partners who understand business models as intimately as consumer psychology.

AI can output fifty decent marketing angles before your morning coffee. Knowing which one is actually worth betting your quarterly budget on is still an entirely human discipline.

Where are you seeing the biggest gap between what AI synthesizes and what actually moves the needle in your strategic decisions?
