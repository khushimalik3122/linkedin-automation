## LinkedIn Post Draft

Google is now placing AI Overviews directly in the Knowledge Panel spot. 

If you thought zero-click search was disruptive before, look at what is actively narrating your company profile right now. 

Search engines aren’t just indexing your B2B positioning anymore; they are synthesizing it. When an AI overview occupies prime Knowledge Panel real estate, prospects don't click through to read your carefully calibrated "About Us" page. They consume an algorithmic summary assembled from third-party reviews, forum mentions, and legacy press coverage.

To maintain positioning control, teams need a three-part operational check:

1. **Entity Auditing:** Regularly prompt and monitor the major LLMs and search engines for your brand name. Track where outdated product specs or dead narratives linger.
2. **Structured Attribution:** Treat your digital footprint as an API for models. Ensure schema markup, corporate registries, and primary PR feeds present clean, machine-readable facts.
3. **Decentralized Proof:** Un-gate high-impact proof points. If your best case studies and ROI metrics are trapped inside PDFs, AI summarizers skip them entirely.

Your brand positioning is no longer what you write on your homepage. It is what the models synthesize when a buyer never clicks.

When was the last time your marketing or executive team audited what search AI states about your core solution?
